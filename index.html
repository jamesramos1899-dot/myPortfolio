01 — about

I'm James Stephen Ramos, a 4th-year Bachelor of Science in Information Technology student at the University of Cebu Lapu-Lapu and Mandaue (UCLM). I wrote my first “Hello, World!” in first year and got hooked — I've been building things ever since.

Every project I've made started with a real, everyday problem, and each one taught me something new: how to design a database, how backend logic connects to what users see, and how to make an interface people can actually use without instructions.

My goal is to grow into a full-stack developer — someone who can take an idea from a rough sketch to a working system. I'm comfortable with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP and MySQL, and I'm currently learning React, Node.js and REST APIs.
